About the shop

A small halal butcher on a big high street.

Spital Hill is one of Sheffield’s most lived-in streets — a stretch of independent grocers, takeaways and family shops serving Burngreave and the streets that climb up from the city centre. Al-Naseem Butcher has its counter here, at number 30.

The job is the same as it’s always been: source fresh meat, cut it properly, sell it across the counter to people who’ll cook it for their families that night. Lamb for the slow-cooker. Goat for the curry. Chicken for the karahi. Mince for the kebab on Friday.

If you live in S4 and you want to know what’s in your meat — drop in. The counter is the answer.

Halal
